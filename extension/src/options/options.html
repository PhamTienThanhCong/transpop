Image translation

Translate text inside images, videos and scanned PDFs — any area of the screen.

How to start
  • Keyboard shortcut Then drag over the text you want to translate.
  • Right-click On an image: “Translate text in image”. Anywhere else: “Translate screen area…”.
  • Toolbar popup Open TransPop and click “Translate screen area” at the top.
