Coffee and calm in Shanghai's old town

By Mai Linh · 4 min read

Tucked between a tailor and a noodle shop, the café is easy to miss. A hand-lettered board by the door tells you everything you need to know — if you can read it.

Inside, the owner pours a matcha latte with the patience of a calligrapher, and the cakes are baked every morning in a kitchen the size of a wardrobe.

Come early: by mid-morning every seat by the window is taken, and the line for take-away stretches out onto the lane.
